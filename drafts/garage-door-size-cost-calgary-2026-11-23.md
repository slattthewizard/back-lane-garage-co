# Garage Door Size and Cost for a Calgary Detached Garage

Most people pick a garage size first and treat the door as a detail. The door is where a lot of the awkward questions land: will the truck clear it, does a bigger door change the wall height, and does one wide door beat two single ones? Getting the garage door size settled before you collect quotes keeps every builder pricing the same thing.

This page covers the door sizes Calgary builders put in their packages, what a 2026 installed door costs from one Calgary door company, and the two City rules that touch the door. ## Common garage door size in Calgary packages

Published packages show what builders treat as the default. These are the door sizes named on the builder pages we read.

- Calgary Garage Builders Ltd lists a 16x7 overhead garage door in its package. That is 16 feet wide by 7 feet high, about 4.9 m by 2.1 m.
- Hammerhead Construction lists a 16'x8' garage door with operator in its all-inclusive garage. That is the same width, one foot taller (about 2.4 m high).
- Corsac Construction does not state its standard door size in the material we read. It does list upgrade options: an 18'x7' or 18'x8' insulated steel overhead door (about 5.5 m wide), and a 9' ceiling. Corsac also says various door sizes are available if tall or wide vehicles will be parked in the garage. No price is given for those upgrades.

Metric figures here are straight conversions from the feet each firm advertises. Other builders do not state a door size on the pages we read, so ask each one directly.

The practical reading: a 16 ft wide door is what these advertised packages show, with 7 or 8 ft of height. Wider or taller is an upgrade, and upgrades are quoted rather than listed.

## How wide a door fits on a double garage

A 16 ft door on a 20 ft wide garage leaves 4 ft of wall in total. If the door is centred, that is about 2 ft (0.6 m) on each side. That is plain arithmetic on the outside width. It does not account for framing, trim or how a builder lays out the front wall, so ask the builder to draw it.

On a 22 ft or 24 ft wide garage the same door leaves 6 ft or 8 ft of wall, which gives room for a side door, a window or a bit of shelving on the inside of that wall. If you are still choosing the garage footprint, the [double garage size guide](/blog/double-garage-size/) compares 20x20, 22x22 and 24x24.

Two single doors are a separate choice. BVM Builders prices its garage door line as an insulated steel overhead door, R-10 minimum for heated garages, with two doors for a double garage. That is BVM's line item for its own builds, at $3,500 to $7,000. Whether one wide door or two narrower ones suits you depends on how you park and how much front wall you want left.

## Do you need an insulated door?

If the garage will not be heated, an uninsulated door is a legitimate choice. If it will be heated, the only number we have is BVM's: R-10 minimum for heated garages. Prime's insulated steel doors are listed at R-12 to R-16, which is above that.

Heating itself is a separate cost. Precision Construction & Decora says a forced-air or radiant tube heater adds $2,500 to $6,000 for a double garage, and insulating and drywalling adds roughly $6,000 to $15,000. A door with a higher insulation value helps a heated garage, but it does not replace insulation in the walls and ceiling. If you plan to work in the garage in winter, tell every builder at the quote stage so the door, walls and heater are priced as a set.

## Tall doors, and the City's 3 m trigger

A taller door usually means taller walls. Corsac lists a 9' ceiling as an add-on. Venkor Group advertises a 9.5' wall height in its packages.

Two City of Calgary rules matter here. The City pages carry a note that the information has no legal status, so confirm anything specific with the City before you build.

First, the City says a development permit is required if the height from the parking surface to where the wall meets the roof truss is higher than 3 metres (9 ft 10 in). A development permit adds a long wait. The City lists 10 to 12 weeks, with no guarantee. A building permit alone is listed at approximately 7 days. So a tall door that pushes the wall past 3 m can add weeks to the schedule. If you want a tall door, ask your builder up front whether the design stays under that line.

Second, the City says the garage must not exceed 4.6 metres overall, and the Land Use Bylaw sets an eaveline limit of 3.0 metres measured from the finished floor. Taller walls use up part of that room.

The permit checklist also asks for beam calculations or manufacturer truss layouts, but the City says these are only needed if the roof trusses are resting on the garage door. If your design puts the trusses over a wide door opening, ask the builder whether those calculations are in the quote.

The [permit guide](/blog/calgary-garage-permit/) covers the full approval path, including when a development permit applies.

## Who connects the door, and which permit covers it

The door also has a permit attached. The City says all new garages need a separate electrical permit for installing lights, outlets and the garage door. Its page on garage permitting says an electrical permit is always needed to install lights and to connect the overhead door.

When an electrical contractor does the work, only a Master Electrician can obtain the electrical permit. A homeowner can apply for a homeowner electrical permit if they own and live in the home and do the work themselves. A new-garage building permit fee is $333.84 in 2026 and a homeowner electrical permit is $116.50, both from the City's 2026 schedule. Contractor trade permits are priced differently.

So when you compare quotes, check that the opener wiring and the electrical permit are inside the price. Corsac says the garage electrical permit and inspection are included on all its detached garages. Other builders may handle it differently, so ask.

## What to ask each builder about the door

Put the same questions to every builder so the quotes can be compared line by line.

- What door size is in the price, in width and height?
- Is the door insulated, and what is its R-value?
- Is an opener included, and is it the basic model or a smart one?
- Is one door or two included on a double?
- What does an 18 ft wide door or a taller door add?
- Does the wall height stay under 3 m from the parking surface to the truss?
- Is the electrical permit for the opener and lights in the price?

A written quote that lists the door by size, insulation and opener is easier to compare than one that says "overhead door included". For the overall package comparison, see [garage packages explained](/blog/garage-packages-explained/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the standard garage door size for a double garage?

Builders in Calgary advertise packages with a 16 ft wide door, either 7 ft high at Calgary Garage Builders Ltd or 8 ft high at Hammerhead Construction. Other builders do not state a size in the material we read. Ask each builder what is in the price.

### Do I need a development permit for a tall garage door?

Not for the door itself. The City asks for a development permit if the height from the parking surface to where the wall meets the roof truss is higher than 3 metres (9 ft 10 in). A tall door can push the wall past that line, so check the design with your builder and confirm with the City.

### How much does a new garage door cost in Calgary?

Prime Garage Doors Calgary advertises installed insulated steel doors at $1,499 to $2,099 for a single and $2,099 to $3,399 for a double in its 2026 guide. A smart Wi-Fi opener starts at $480 installed. Builders price doors inside their packages differently, so ask for the door line in writing.

### Does connecting the garage door need an electrical permit?

Yes. The City says a new detached garage always needs an electrical permit for lights and to connect the overhead door. When an electrical contractor does the work, only a Master Electrician can obtain that permit.
