# Triple Car Garage Cost in Calgary: Prices, Slab Rules and Lot Limits

A triple garage is the point where a detached garage stops being a standard package. The slab needs engineering, the lot has to be big enough, and the price jumps well past a double. Most of the surprises come from the rules, not the lumber.

This page uses prices that Calgary builders publish and rules the City publishes, so you know what to ask before you collect quotes. If you would rather talk it through first, [call (403) 555-0162](tel:+14035550162). Independent Calgary garage builders quote and contract with you directly.

## Triple car garage cost: what builders publish

Two Calgary firms publish a triple package price. They are starting prices for a standard wood-frame garage, not finished costs, and they are not a quote from this site.

| Firm | Size | Advertised price | GST |
|---|---|---|---|
| Venkor Group | 30x24 | $48,606 | not stated |
| Corsac Construction | 36x20 | $49,000 | + GST |

Both sizes work out to 720 sq ft, or about 66.9 m2. That is arithmetic on the nominal dimensions. The shapes are very different. A 30x24 is a wider, deeper block. A 36x20 is a long, narrow building with three bays in a row and a shallower depth.

For scale, the same firms advertise a 24x24 double at $34,988 (Venkor, GST not stated) and $29,900 plus GST (Corsac). Going by those published prices, the triple adds $13,618 at Venkor and $19,100 plus GST at Corsac. Builders structure these steps differently, so price the double and the triple from the same firm side by side.

What the package includes matters. Venkor lists a 4 inch slab with a 6 inch curb wall, 2x4 studs, vinyl siding, 9.5 foot walls and a 240V 30A subpanel, and says City permits and design drawings are included in the quote. Corsac lists a 4 to 5 inch concrete pad with a 6 inch curb, 2x4 studs, 8 foot walls and a 40-amp service with a dedicated subpanel. For the triple it says "engineer pad" and notes that stamped structural drawings are required. Ask any builder whether the engineering is inside the package price or added to it.

## Finished triple garage ranges

Package prices are the low end. Cost guides from other builders describe a finished building and run much higher. Treat each as what that firm advertises, not a market figure.

- Precision Construction & Decora gives $90,000 to $140,000 or more for an oversized, triple or tall-door garage, 26 feet wide or more. It calls these typical market ranges, not fixed prices from that firm. GST is not stated.
- BVM Builders, in a 2026 cost guide, gives a triple at $105,000 to $140,000 unheated and $120,000 to $168,000 heated. GST is not stated.
- BVM's own garage-construction page gives different numbers for a 33x24 triple: $65,000 to $95,000 for a basic build and $100,000 to $145,000 for a premium one. BVM's pages do not match each other, so read each as BVM's own advertised range and nothing more.
- Polskiy Building lists a three-car garage in Okotoks from $44,800 plus GST. That is an Okotoks price, not a Calgary one.
- BVM's Airdrie page lists a 32x26 triple at $135,000 to $200,000. That is an Airdrie price and should not be read as a Calgary figure.

The distance between about $49,000 and $140,000 is the distance between a package and a finished garage. The post on [what it costs to build a garage in Calgary](/blog/cost-to-build-a-garage-calgary/) walks through what moves the number.

## Why a 30x24 passes the 55 m2 engineered slab line

The City's garage page says that if a garage slab is bigger than 55 m2 (592 sq ft) and does not have a concrete foundation 1.2 m below grade, you need a concrete slab designed by a professional engineer. A 30x24 is about 66.9 m2. A 36x20 is the same. Both are over the line, which is why Corsac writes "engineer pad" on its triple and requires stamped structural drawings.

The City's Regulation Bulletin RB14-011 quotes the Alberta code on this. Detached garages under 55 m2 and one storey high may sit on a 100 mm concrete floor slab, provided the garage is not masonry or masonry veneer. The same bulletin lets a larger garage sit on a slab if it is structurally compartmentalised into 55 m2 sections, with no plates, trusses, beams or rebar crossing the compartment line. Whether a builder uses an engineered slab, a foundation below the frost line or a compartmentalised design is something to ask in writing. Corsac states that slabs over 55 m2 require an engineered foundation design.

This is where the cost goes. Precision Construction & Decora says a standard double-garage slab with the right footings runs $9,000 to $18,000 of the total. BVM puts a total concrete package for a double at $12,500 to $22,000, and its specification includes footings at 1.2 m. Neither figure is for a triple, and a triple has a larger area to pour and engineer. Ask each builder to price the slab separately.

Rocky View County offers pre-engineered slab-on-grade designs for eligible single-storey detached garages. A garage with a second floor or mezzanine needs a site-specific engineered foundation. The Okotoks garage form says a professional engineer or architect is required if the structure exceeds 55 m2. If your lot is in a town or county, check its rules as well as the City's.

## The 75 m2 development permit trigger and coverage limits

A detached garage over 10 m2 (107 sq ft) needs a building permit. The City lists the new-garage fee for 2026 at $333.84. A new garage also needs a separate electrical permit. A garage that meets every Land Use Bylaw rule needs those permits and no development permit.

A development permit is added when the garage is greater than 75 m2 (807 sq ft), higher than 4.6 m, taller than 3 m from the parking surface to where the wall meets the roof truss, larger than the existing house, or closer than 1.0 m to the house. Any bylaw relaxation also needs one. The City lists a development permit at $550 for a proposal that relaxes the bylaw, with a timeline of 10 to 12 weeks, against about 7 days for a building permit. Those timelines are not guaranteed. A 30x24 at about 66.9 m2 is under the 75 m2 trigger. A 33x24 is about 73.6 m2, also under it. A few more feet of length and you are over.

A second limit catches more triples. All accessory buildings on the parcel together must not exceed the lesser of the main house's building coverage or 75 m2 per dwelling unit. Buildings totalling 10 m2 or less are left out of the count. Take 66.9 m2 from 75 m2 and about 8.1 m2 is left for everything else, if the house footprint is at least 75 m2. A small house footprint lowers the cap further, so a smaller house can rule out a triple even on a wide lot.

Parcel coverage applies too. The City lists total coverage by district, from 40 per cent to 45 per cent in common low-density districts, with other figures elsewhere. The City's own worked example is a 331.52 m2 R-C1 lot with a house, deck, shed and a proposed 40.8 m2 garage, which reaches 41.47 per cent. Swap that garage for a 66.9 m2 triple and, by plain arithmetic, the total rises to about 49 per cent, over the 45 per cent limit. That is an arithmetic check on the City's example, not a statement about your lot.

Setbacks and distances also take width. The side or rear setback is 0.6 m, or 1.2 m where the line is shared with a street, and the garage must be at least 1.0 m from the house. A 36x20 is about 11.0 m long. Add 0.6 m at each end and you need about 12.2 m of lot dimension before anything else. That is arithmetic as well. A Real Property Report from an Alberta land surveyor is the usual way to see what your lot allows, and the [Calgary garage permit guide](/blog/calgary-garage-permit/) covers who applies and what the City asks for.

These pages carry a disclaimer that they have no legal status, so confirm every point with the City of Calgary.

## Triple garages outside the city

Rules change at the edge of Calgary, and triples often land in large-lot areas. BVM says triple garages are most popular in large-lot suburban communities including Springbank Hill, Aspen Woods and Rocky View County. That is a marketing claim, not a statistic.

- Cochrane. No development permit is needed for an accessory building of 53.5 m2 or less and 4.6 m or less in height. A 66.9 m2 triple is over it. Combined accessory building lot coverage is capped at 20 per cent.
- Chestermere. A garage is defined as over 10 m2 but not over 50 m2. Anything larger falls under "Accessory Building, Other", which is discretionary in the R-1 district. R-1 accessory coverage is 10 per cent, so 66.9 m2 would need a lot of at least 669 m2 by arithmetic.
- Okotoks. The garage form requires an engineer or architect above 55 m2.
- Rocky View County. The 2026 rate bylaw lists building permit fees per square foot, at $0.30 under 592 sq ft and $0.40 over it, plus a 4 per cent levy. All truss layouts must be stamped by an Alberta engineer under rules effective 1 March 2026.

## Is a triple the right call?

A triple is worth pricing when the lot is wide, the house footprint supports the coverage cap, and you want a bay for a second vehicle, a project or storage. If the lot is tight, a 24x24 double is a smaller step. The post on [double garage size](/blog/double-garage-size/) covers it. For anything wider or taller than a standard package, see the [custom garages page](/custom-garages/).

Ask every builder to quote the same size, the same wall height, the same number of doors and the same electrical service, with slab engineering broken out. A builder who sells at your home and takes money before the work is done needs a prepaid contracting licence, which you can check on Service Alberta's [licence search](https://www.servicealberta.ca/find-if-business-is-licenced.cfm). A licence is not an endorsement of quality.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does a triple car garage cost in Calgary?

Published package prices start near $48,606 at Venkor Group for a 30x24 and $49,000 plus GST at Corsac Construction for a 36x20. Finished cost guides from other builders run from $90,000 to $140,000 or more. Each figure is what that firm advertises, so written quotes show your actual number.

### Does a triple garage need an engineered slab in Calgary?

A 30x24 is about 66.9 m2. The City says a slab over 55 m2 without a concrete foundation 1.2 m below grade needs a design by a professional engineer. RB14-011 also allows a design compartmentalised into 55 m2 sections. Your builder and the City decide which applies.

### Do I need a development permit for a triple garage?

Not by size alone if it is 75 m2 (807 sq ft) or less. A 30x24 at about 66.9 m2 is under that line. A development permit is still needed for the other triggers the City lists, such as height over 4.6 m or a location closer than 1.0 m to the house.

### Will a triple garage fit on my lot?

It depends on the setbacks, the accessory building cap and the parcel coverage limit for your district. All accessory buildings together cannot exceed the lesser of the house's building coverage or 75 m2. Your Real Property Report and the City will show what the lot allows.
