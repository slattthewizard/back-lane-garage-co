# Garage Quote Comparison: How to Compare Calgary Builders Line by Line

Two garage quotes for the same 22x22 can sit thousands of dollars apart and still be the cheaper and the dearer of two very different buildings. The headline number is the least useful part of a quote. What decides whether you are comparing like with like is the list of inclusions underneath it.

This page shows how to line quotes up, using what Calgary firms publish on their own sites. Every price below is what that firm advertises, not a market average and not a price from this site. ## Why headline prices do not compare

Take the 20x20 double. Venkor Group publishes $25,190 and does not say whether GST is included. Corsac Construction publishes $25,900 plus GST. Calgary Garage Builders Ltd publishes $25,500 plus GST. As plain arithmetic, Corsac is $710 above Venkor on the face of it, but one price has GST stated and one does not, so the gap could be larger or smaller.

Even if the sizes and the GST lined up, the buildings would not. The firms publish different slab thicknesses, wall heights, door sizes and electrical services. A cheaper quote with an 8 foot wall and a 15 amp service is not the same product as a dearer one with a 9.5 foot wall and a 30 amp subpanel.

## The line items to match

Build one sheet with the same rows for every builder, and fill it from the written quote, not from the website or a phone call.

- Size, outside dimensions, and whether the quote is for the exact lot.
- Slab thickness, thickening or footings, curb height, and what is done to the base.
- Wall height and stud size.
- Siding type and roof finish.
- Door size, number of doors, insulation, and whether an opener is included.
- Electrical: amperage, whether it is 240V, whether there is a subpanel, number of outlets and lights.
- Permits: which ones, who applies, who pays, and whether the design drawings are included.
- GST: shown as a separate line or said to be included.
- Site work: grading, excavation, removal of an old garage or slab.
- Heat and insulation, if you want them.
- Payment schedule, and what you pay at signing.

The City's building permit for a new garage is $333.84 in 2026, including the Safety Codes Council fee, and a homeowner electrical permit is $116.50. City pages say they have no legal status, so confirm fees with the City. If a quote lists permits as a large line, ask how it is built up. The [Calgary garage permit guide](/blog/calgary-garage-permit/) covers what the City asks for.

## Conflicting prices from the same firm

Some firms publish more than one number, and the numbers disagree. Treat that as a reason to ask for a written quote.

V AAR Exteriors is the clearest case. A page dated 21 December 2025 says 20x20 starts at $20,000, 20x22 at $21,000, 22x22 at $22,000, 22x24 at $23,000 and 24x24 at $24,000 and more. A post dated 11 March 2026 lists $16,000, $16,500, $17,000, $18,000 and $19,000 for the same five sizes. That is $4,000 less per size on the later page, and neither page says GST. The March post also links a triple garage at $20,000 without a concrete pad.

Venkor's own page shows $20,861 on the double-car package card, the same as its single, while its price table shows $25,190 for 20x20. Corsac's double-car card shows $23,800 against $25,900 plus GST in its size table. In both cases the size tables are the better guide, but ask each builder which number is the quote.

Alta Home advertises a package starting at $16,950, and its pricing page covers Calgary and Edmonton together with ranges that do not match. A "starting at" price is an opening figure, not a total.

## Add-ons to normalize

Add-ons are where a low quote often catches up. Put each builder's add-on prices on your sheet, and ask for the figures you are missing.

- Electrical upgrade. Calgary Garage Builders Ltd lists a 60 amp upgrade at $2,000 plus GST on its pricing page. A separate cost page gives approximately $1,500. Polskiy Building, in a list labelled Okotoks, lists 240V subpanel upgrades of $900 for 40A, $1,000 for 50A and $1,200 for 60A plus GST. That list is for an Okotoks page, not a Calgary price.
- Doors. Corsac lists 18x7 and 18x8 insulated steel doors and a 9 foot ceiling as options, with no price given. Falcon Contracting says premium doors add $600 to $1,800 over standard doors. Prime Garage Doors Calgary lists an installed insulated steel double at $2,099 to $3,399.
- Heat. Corsac says its gas heater package is $6,600, including heater, underground line, trenching, permitting and dedicated electrical. Polskiy lists a 50,000 BTU gas heater at $2,000 plus GST, and a gas line with permits at $3,000 plus GST, in its Okotoks list.
- Demolition. Corsac says removing an existing detached garage starts at $17.50 per square foot.

A worked example, labelled as arithmetic only. Calgary Garage Builders Ltd's 20x20 at $25,500 plus GST, with its 60 amp upgrade at $2,000 plus GST, comes to $27,500 plus GST. Corsac's 22x22 is $27,500 plus GST with a 40 amp service. Those two are not the same size, the same electrical service or the same inclusions, which is the whole point of normalizing.

Where an add-on has no published price, such as a taller wall or a bigger door, ask every builder to price it in the same quote. Sizes themselves are covered in the [double garage size guide](/blog/double-garage-size/).

## Deposit and licence questions

Ask three things before any money moves. How much is due at signing? What triggers each later payment? What is the builder's licence number?

Alberta does not publish a deposit cap that this site could find, so the amount is a matter for the contract. Service Alberta's prepaid contracting licence applies to a business that sells in person away from its own premises and takes money before all the work is done. You can look a business up on the Service Alberta [licence search](https://www.servicealberta.ca/find-if-business-is-licenced.cfm). A licence is not an endorsement of quality, and Service Alberta says so.

If you sign at home, the Consumer Protection Act gives a 10-day cancellation right for direct sales contracts, counted from the day you receive your copy. That is a reason to ask for the written contract promptly.

## Ask every builder for the same spec

The most useful step is also the simplest. Write one specification and send it to each builder.

1. Pick the size, wall height, door size, electrical service and slab.
2. Say who applies for the permits and ask for the permit cost as its own line.
3. Ask for GST shown separately.
4. Ask for the payment schedule and the licence number.
5. Ask what is excluded.

Then compare the totals. The [cost guide](/blog/cost-to-build-a-garage-calgary/) explains what moves the price, and [garage packages explained](/blog/garage-packages-explained/) describes how packages are put together.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many garage quotes should I get?

Two or three written quotes give you enough to see where the real differences are. Ask each builder to price the same size, wall height, door and electrical service so the totals line up.

### Why do garage quotes in Calgary differ so much?

Firms publish different inclusions. Slab thickness, wall height, door size, electrical service, permits and GST treatment all vary, and some firms publish more than one price for the same size. A written quote on one spec removes most of the gap.

### Should a garage quote include permits?

It depends on the builder. Venkor says City permits and design drawings are included, and Corsac says its garage electrical permit and inspection are included. Ask who applies and whether the permit cost is a separate line.

### Is the lowest garage quote the best one?

Not by itself. A lower price can mean thinner inclusions, a smaller electrical service or GST left out. Compare the inclusions, the add-on prices, the payment schedule and the licence before you decide.
