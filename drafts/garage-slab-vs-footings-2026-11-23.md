# Garage Slab vs Footings in Calgary: What the Rules Say and What Builders Charge

The foundation is the part of a detached garage you cannot change later. It is also the part where builders quote the most different numbers, because some price a simple floating slab and others price footings dug to frost depth. Comparing those two as if they were the same product is how two foundation quotes thousands of dollars apart end up on the same table.

This page sets out what the City of Calgary says about garage slabs, what builders advertise, and the questions to ask in writing. If you would rather talk it through first, [call (403) 555-0162](tel:+14035550162). Independent Calgary garage builders quote and contract with you directly.

## Two ways to build a garage foundation

A floating slab on grade is a concrete floor poured on prepared ground, without footings that reach below the frost line. A frost-depth foundation puts footings and walls down to a depth below where the ground freezes, with the floor poured inside.

Calgary's building rules allow the first for many garages. City Regulation Bulletin RB14-011, which quotes the Alberta Building Code, says detached garages of less than 55 m2 floor area and not more than one storey high may be supported on wood mud sills or a 100 mm thick concrete floor slab, provided the garage is not masonry or masonry veneer. That is the code language the City republished in 2015 against the 2014 code, so confirm the current edition with the City and your builder. The City's pages say they have no legal status.

Builders repeat this. Precision Construction & Decora says that for a standard detached garage under 55 m2, the Alberta Building Code allows a properly built floating slab on grade. Corsac Construction lists a 4 to 5 inch concrete pad with a 6 inch curb on its packages. Venkor Group lists a 4 inch slab with a 6 inch curb wall. Hammerhead Construction lists a 4 inch slab with a 10 inch thickening.

Some builders go deeper by choice. BVM Builders specifies perimeter footings 12 inches wide and 10 inches deep, set at least 1.2 m below grade, which it describes as Calgary frost depth, on its own builds. That is BVM's specification, not a code requirement for a garage under 55 m2. The City states a frost depth of 4 feet or deeper only for backyard suites, which are a different building type with different approvals.

## The 55 m2 rule

The City's garage page puts the line this way. If your garage slab is bigger than 55 m2 (592 sq ft), and does not have a concrete foundation 1.2 m below grade, you need a concrete slab designed by a professional engineer.

Here is what that means in sizes, using plain arithmetic on nominal dimensions.

- 20x20 is 400 sq ft, about 37.2 m2.
- 24x24 is 576 sq ft, about 53.5 m2, just under the line.
- 26x26 is 676 sq ft, about 62.8 m2, over it.
- 30x24 is 720 sq ft, about 66.9 m2, over it.

Corsac says slabs over 55 m2 require an engineered foundation design, and on its 36x20 triple it lists an "engineer pad" with stamped structural drawings. There are two other ways past the line.

RB14-011 lets a larger garage sit on a slab if it is structurally compartmentalised into 55 m2 sections. That means no plates, trusses, beams or rebar crossing the line between compartments. It is a design choice that your builder and the City have to agree on. The other route is a foundation that reaches 1.2 m below grade, as the City page describes.

The exact outside dimensions decide which rule applies, and a few inches can matter near the line. Ask the builder to state the slab area in square metres on the quote.

## Why Calgary soil comes up

Builders often mention ground movement. Precision Construction & Decora says Calgary's clay soil and freeze-thaw cycles mean the foundation has to be built to handle ground movement. That is a builder statement, not a City finding, and it is not a soil report for your lot. It is a reason to ask what the builder does about drainage, base preparation and thickened edges, and whether anything differs on your site.

Calgary Garage Builders Ltd lists a slab with proper base preparation in its package. Hammerhead says garages can be built in cooler months but the concrete needs winter heat to pour, which can add to the cost. Falcon Contracting says winter builds are possible with specialized concrete techniques and may add slight heating costs. Ask whether a winter pour is priced in.

Other site conditions affect the slab as well. The City says permanent structures, including any footing, wall or eave, are not permitted in a utility right-of-way. Provincial regulations prohibit building a garage over a gas line, so the garage must move at least 0.3 m from the line or the line must be sleeved. That City list is dated 2016, so confirm it now. An existing parking pad needs an assessment before garage construction, according to AB Garage Builders. The City says a building permit is not required for a parking pad itself, but if you plan to pour one for a future garage you should think about setbacks and design rules first.

When you compare quotes, ask each builder for four things in writing.

- Slab thickness, edge thickening and curb height.
- Whether footings are included, and to what depth.
- The slab area in square metres, and whether it is over 55 m2.
- Whether engineering is included or extra, and whether the quote assumes any soil work.

## What the builder and the City each handle

Do not read this page as a design guide. Each point depends on your lot. The City says a new detached garage over 10 m2 needs a building permit, and that if you hire someone to do the work they apply for the permit. The 2026 City fee for a new garage is $333.84. An architect or professional engineer may be required to design and inspect a project, and the City refers to the Alberta code on that. Drawings stamped "for permit purposes only" or "preliminary" are not acceptable.

In most cases the City requires a framing inspection. The garage must be at lock-up stage with doors and windows installed. The post on [the Calgary garage permit](/blog/calgary-garage-permit/) covers who applies and what is asked for. For the City's own wording, see [Regulation Bulletin RB14-011](https://www.calgary.ca/content/dam/www/pda/pd/documents/building/regulation_bulletins/2014-bulletins/regulation-bulletin-rb14-011.pdf).

## Slabs in Rocky View County, Okotoks and other towns

The City's rules are not the only ones. If your garage is outside Calgary, check the local authority.

- Rocky View County. The county offers pre-engineered slab-on-grade foundation designs for eligible single-storey detached garages. A garage with a second floor or mezzanine needs a site-specific engineered foundation design. Its 2026 rates bylaw lists building permit fees per square foot, $0.30 for accessory buildings under 592 sq ft (55 m2) and $0.40 over it, plus a 4 per cent levy. See [garage builders in Rocky View County](/garage-builders-rocky-view-county/).
- Okotoks. The Town's 2025 garage form says a professional engineer or architect is required if the garage structure exceeds 55 m2. It also says windows and doors are not permitted within 1.2 m of a property line. See [garage builders in Okotoks](/garage-builders-okotoks/).
- Cochrane, Chestermere and Airdrie. Their size triggers and fees differ, and the City of Airdrie requires an Alberta engineer's seal on truss specifications from 1 March 2026. The slab rule is a separate question. Ask the builder which authority applies to your address and what it requires for the foundation.

## How to choose before you collect quotes

If your garage is under 55 m2, a floating slab is a recognised option under the code the City quotes, and many builders price it. A foundation to frost depth costs more and is the builder's specification choice, not a requirement for that size. If your garage is over 55 m2, the engineering question comes first, and the price should show what the builder has chosen.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a detached garage in Calgary sit on a floating slab?

The City's Regulation Bulletin RB14-011 quotes the code as allowing a garage under 55 m2 and one storey high to sit on a 100 mm concrete floor slab, if it is not masonry or masonry veneer. Confirm the current rule with the City and your builder before you rely on it.

### Do I need footings for a 24x24 garage?

A 24x24 is about 53.5 m2, just under the 55 m2 line. The code language the City quotes allows a slab for garages under that size. Some builders, such as BVM Builders, specify footings at 1.2 m on their own builds as a choice, so ask what each quote includes.

### What happens if my garage slab is over 55 m2?

The City says a slab over 55 m2 without a concrete foundation 1.2 m below grade needs a design by a professional engineer. RB14-011 also allows a design compartmentalised into 55 m2 sections. Your builder and the City decide which route applies.

### How much does a garage slab cost in Calgary?

Patriarch Construction says most pads fall between $4,500 and $8,000. Builders that include footings advertise more, such as Precision Construction & Decora at $9,000 to $18,000 and BVM Builders at $12,500 to $22,000 for a double. Written quotes show your price.
