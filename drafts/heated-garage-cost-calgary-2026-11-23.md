# Heated Garage Cost in Calgary: Insulation, Heat, Electrical and Permits

The package listings Calgary builders publish do not mention insulation. Heating one is a second project with its own line items: insulation and drywall, a heater, a gas line or an electrical upgrade, a better door and extra permits. The published numbers for those pieces are far apart, which is why one builder's heated price looks nothing like another's.

This page lays out what Calgary builders advertise for each piece, plus the City rules that apply. If you would rather talk it through first, [call (403) 555-0162](tel:+14035550162). Independent Calgary garage builders quote and contract with you directly.

## What builders say heating adds

Start with the totals. Each is what that firm advertises, not a market average and not a quote from this site.

- Precision Construction & Decora (PCND) says insulating and drywalling the walls and ceiling adds roughly $6,000 to $15,000 for a double, and a forced-air or radiant tube heater adds $2,500 to $6,000. In-floor heat is an extra $6,000 to $12,000. GST is not stated.
- BVM Builders says heating adds $8,000 to $22,000 to a standard double. It lists a gas unit heater at $3,500 to $6,000 installed and in-floor hydronic heat at $12,000 to $22,000. GST is not stated.
- Falcon Contracting says to add $2,000 to $5,000 for insulation and heating systems on a heated workshop. GST is not stated.
- Corsac Construction says its current price for a garage gas heater package is $6,600, which includes the heater, the underground line, trenching, permitting and dedicated electrical services.

BVM also publishes heated and unheated ranges side by side. A 14x24 single is $45,000 to $62,000 unheated and $55,000 to $75,000 heated. A 22x24 double is $62,000 to $90,000 unheated and $72,000 to $100,000 heated. A triple is $105,000 to $140,000 unheated and $120,000 to $168,000 heated. By subtraction, the low end of each range rises by $10,000 for the single and the double and $15,000 for the triple. BVM's pages do not all agree with each other, so treat these as BVM's advertised figures.

The spread is wide because the scopes differ. Falcon's $2,000 to $5,000 and PCND's $6,000 to $15,000 for insulation alone are not the same job. Ask what each number includes before you compare. The post on [what it costs to build a garage in Calgary](/blog/cost-to-build-a-garage-calgary/) puts heat in the context of the full price, and [garage packages explained](/blog/garage-packages-explained/) shows what a base package usually leaves out.

## Insulation and drywall

Venkor, Corsac and Calgary Garage Builders Ltd all publish package prices for a standard garage, and none of those package listings mentions insulation. Heat starts with the walls and ceiling.

Polskiy Building publishes add-on prices on its Okotoks page, which are Okotoks prices and not Calgary ones. It lists insulation and poly vapour barrier at $1,700 plus GST, and drywall and insulation at $3,000 plus GST. That is one builder, in one town, and the garage size behind those figures is not stated on the page. Compare it with PCND's $6,000 to $15,000 range for a double and you can see how much the scope and size change the number.

Two rules affect drywall in Calgary. The City says 5/8 inch Type X drywall is required from floor to ceiling on all interior walls within 0.6 m (1.9 ft) of the side property line. That applies to accessory buildings of 10 m2 or more. At the framing inspection, fire rating standards must be complete with the fire-rated materials installed. If your garage sits close to the fence, Type X is required whether or not you heat it.

The City also lists eave and soffit rules near property lines, such as a non-vented soffit within 1.2 m. Ask the builder to confirm how the garage meets those rules, and confirm with the City, since its pages state they have no legal status.

## The garage door

A heated garage needs a door that holds heat. BVM lists an insulated steel overhead door with an R-10 minimum for heated garages, with two doors for a double, at $3,500 to $7,000. Prime Garage Doors Calgary, in its 2026 price guide, lists installed insulated steel doors rated R-12 to R-16 at $1,499 to $2,099 for a single and $2,099 to $3,399 for a double. GST is not stated for either.

The two figures come from different sources and different scopes, so do not treat them as one market price. Falcon says premium doors add $600 to $1,800 versus standard doors. A door is easy to under-budget, so ask whether the quote includes an insulated door and an opener.

## Gas heater, in-floor heat and electric options

A gas unit heater is the lowest published route. PCND puts a forced-air or radiant tube heater at $2,500 to $6,000. BVM lists a gas unit heater at $3,500 to $6,000 installed. Corsac advertises a $6,600 gas package that bundles the line, trenching, permit and electrical. Polskiy lists a 50,000 BTU gas heater at $2,000 plus GST and a gas line with permits, where available, at $3,000 plus GST. By arithmetic, those two Okotoks items together come to $5,000 plus GST, or $8,000 plus GST with Polskiy's drywall and insulation add-on. That sum is not a price from Polskiy.

In-floor heat is the high end. PCND says to budget an extra $6,000 to $12,000. BVM lists in-floor hydronic at $12,000 to $22,000. In-floor heat is built into the concrete, so decide on it before the slab is poured.

Gas lines carry a site rule. Provincial regulations prohibit building a garage over a gas line. On the City's older list, the garage must be moved at least 0.3 m from the line or the line must be sleeved with a gas tight sleeve. That list is dated 2016, so confirm it with the City.

No published Calgary price for an electric heater came up in the sources read for this page, so ask builders to price one if you do not want a gas line. Either way, the electrical question below applies.

## Electrical for a heated garage

Every new garage needs a separate electrical permit for lights, outlets and the overhead door. Heating can raise the load. Published figures vary.

- Falcon says basic electrical service runs $800 to $1,500, and an EV charging station adds $1,200 to $2,500.
- PCND says a 240V outlet for tools or an EV charger, a sub-panel or extra lighting costs $2,000 to $6,000.
- BVM's standard package with a 100-amp sub-panel is listed at $5,000 to $9,000.
- Calgary Garage Builders Ltd lists a 60-amp upgrade at $2,000 plus GST on its pricing page.
- Polskiy lists 240V sub-panel upgrades in Okotoks at $900 for 40A, $1,000 for 50A and $1,200 for 60A, plus GST.

Package electrical varies too. Corsac lists a 40-amp service with a dedicated subpanel, Venkor a 240V 30A subpanel, and Calgary Garage Builders Ltd a 15-amp service. Ask the builder to state the amperage and whether it is enough for a heater and the loads you plan.

## Gas and electrical permits

The City lists a gas permit for a new gas line for a heater as an example of a trade permit a detached garage may need. A homeowner can pull a homeowner gas permit only if they hold a first-class or second-class gas fitter journeyman certificate and own and live in the home. Otherwise a contractor pulls the permit. When an electrical contractor does the work, only a Master Electrician can obtain the electrical permit.

Homeowners who do their own electrical work can apply for a homeowner electrical permit at $116.50 with the Safety Codes fee, but the City says you cannot pull a permit on behalf of someone else doing the work. A homeowner may add a sub-panel to an existing main service that has a main breaker, and may not alter the main service, main panel or meter base. Contractor trade permits cost $112 plus $9.79 per $1,000 of construction value, plus the 4 per cent levy.

The building permit for a new garage is $333.84 in 2026. Starting work before receiving a permit doubles the fee. Cochrane lists a gas permit for a garage heater at $90 plus the 4 per cent levy and requires a certified gas fitter to obtain a gas permit, but that is Cochrane. For Calgary, the City's [garage permits page](https://www.calgary.ca/development/home-building/garages-sheds.html) is the place to confirm, and the post on the [Calgary garage permit](/blog/calgary-garage-permit/) covers who applies. See also the page for [garage builders in Okotoks](/garage-builders-okotoks/) if you are building there.

Concealed wiring needs care. A homeowner permit cannot be issued for concealed wiring done without proper permits, and the inspector may ask for drywall to be removed. Finish the electrical inspection before the drywall goes up.

## Putting a heated garage budget together

A practical way to build the budget is to price each piece separately and add them.

- Base package, with slab, framing, roof and a basic door.
- Insulation, vapour barrier and drywall, including Type X where the walls are within 0.6 m of a side line.
- An insulated door, with R-10 or better if heating.
- A heater and its fuel supply, whether gas line and permit or electrical upgrade.
- Electrical service sized for the heater and your tools.
- Trade permits, which the contractor or you must pull as the City describes.

Then ask the builder to state which of those are in the package and which are extra. Heating is also a decision about use. A heated workshop earns its cost differently from a garage that only needs to stay above freezing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does it cost to heat a detached garage in Calgary?

Published figures vary by scope. Precision Construction & Decora says insulation and drywall add roughly $6,000 to $15,000 for a double and a heater $2,500 to $6,000. BVM Builders says heating adds $8,000 to $22,000, and Corsac Construction advertises a $6,600 gas heater package. Each is what that firm advertises.

### Do I need a gas permit to install a garage heater in Calgary?

The City lists a gas permit for a new gas line for a heater as an example of a trade permit a detached garage may need. A homeowner can pull one only with a first-class or second-class gas fitter journeyman certificate and if they own and live in the home. Confirm the details with the City.

### Does a heated garage need a special door?

BVM Builders lists an insulated steel door with an R-10 minimum for heated garages. Prime Garage Doors Calgary lists installed insulated steel doors from R-12 to R-16. Ask your builder what rating the quoted door has and whether it is included.

### Do I need Type X drywall in my garage?

The City requires 5/8 inch Type X drywall on all interior walls within 0.6 m of the side property line, for accessory buildings of 10 m2 or more. The rule applies whether or not the garage is heated. Your builder and the City confirm how it applies to your lot.
